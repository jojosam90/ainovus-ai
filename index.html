About the company

We sit where artificial intelligence meets the oldest human habit — a good story.

Founded in Singapore, Ainovus Pte. Ltd. sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and short-form entertainment. We use AI to produce original short dramas faster and at scale — then stream them on our own platform built for Southeast Asian audiences.

We also share what we know. Our AI video training courses teach creators, filmmakers and entrepreneurs how to harness AI tools to produce compelling short drama — without a traditional production budget.

Ainovus Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based AI short drama company with three core products: (1) AI Video Production — we use AI to produce original short drama content at scale; (2) AI Video Training Courses — we teach creators to produce short drama with AI tools; and (3) a Short Drama Streaming Platform — the Ainovus app, where viewers watch AI-produced dramas across Southeast Asia.
AI video production uses artificial intelligence to generate short drama content — from writing scripts and creating characters to generating video scenes, adding voiceover, music, and subtitles. At Ainovus, we use this to produce high-quality short drama series much faster and more cost-effectively than traditional film production.
Our AI video training course covers the full short drama production pipeline: Module 1 — AI scriptwriting; Module 2 — AI video generation; Module 3 — voice, audio, and sound design with AI; Module 4 — publishing and monetisation. It's designed for creators, filmmakers, and entrepreneurs. No prior production experience required.
